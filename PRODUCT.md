# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, engineering leaders, founders, clients, and fellow engineers reviewing Jatin Dahiya's work, often with only a few minutes to understand his background and technical range. They need to quickly identify who he is, what he does, where he has worked, what he has built, the technologies he uses, and how to contact him.

## Product Purpose

Present Jatin Dahiya as a software and systems engineer who builds practical products across web, desktop, data, and applied AI. The portfolio should make his experience and projects easy to assess while demonstrating unusually strong frontend engineering, interaction design, motion craft, accessibility, and attention to detail through the site itself.

## Brand Personality

Precise, inventive, and assured. The voice is technically literate and human, confident without exaggeration. The emotional result should feel like a carefully maintained digital workshop: focused, tactile, capable, and full of quiet details worth discovering.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a generic Tailwind landing page, SaaS dashboard, fake terminal, neon cyberpunk interface, glassmorphism collection, purple-blue gradient site, endless bento grid, or animation demo. Avoid fabricated metrics, employers, awards, testimonials, results, technologies, and case-study claims. Do not copy the layouts, typography, cursor, or signature interactions of the named inspiration sites.

## Design Principles

1. Make the work the proof. Project imagery and truthful technical detail carry more weight than self-promotional claims.
2. Clarity before spectacle. A visitor with thirty seconds can identify Jatin, his role, current experience, strongest projects, and contact path.
3. Motion explains and connects. Each interaction has a reason tied to hierarchy, navigation, or project character.
4. Build one coherent workshop. Typography, spacing, color, imagery, and microinteractions feel authored as a system rather than assembled from trends.
5. Reward attention without requiring it. Small details add personality, while every core action remains obvious on keyboard, touch, and reduced-motion settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Use semantic HTML, logical heading order, visible focus states, complete keyboard navigation, large touch targets, meaningful alternative text, strong contrast, reduced-motion fallbacks, focus-managed mobile navigation, and no information or action available only on hover. Support narrow mobile screens from 320px and preserve usability without animation.
